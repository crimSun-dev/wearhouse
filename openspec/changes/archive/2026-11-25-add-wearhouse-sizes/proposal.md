## Why

Clothes come in sizes, and a wardrobe and store are not believable without them. Shoppers need to:
- filter the store to what fits them;
- pick a size when buying;
- see the size on what they own.

The owner asked for the usual commercial sizes, a per-user "usual size", and size-based sorting.

The H&M articles file has **no size column**. Product sizes are therefore mock data, generated by a rule and editable by staff, and labelled as such. Size scales and sizes are data objects that staff can add and retire, never hardcoded lists (the owner's object-management direction).

## What Changes

- **Size registry (catalogue).** `size_system` and `size_value` tables. They are seeded with:
  - Letter (international) XXS–3XL;
  - EU clothing 32–52;
  - EU shoe 35–47.

  Each system lists the wardrobe slots it applies to by default, and each size has a rank (for sorting) and a "common" flag (for default assignment). Staff with `catalogue:ingest` can add and edit systems and sizes, and retire them; retired values stay readable for history.
- **Product sizes (catalogue).** Each product is offered in sizes from one system, held in `product_size` with an `origin` of `rule` or `staff`:
  - staff can set or clear a product's sizes;
  - "assign missing" (endpoint and CLI) gives every published product without sizes the common sizes of the system its slot defaults to; accessories stay one-size.

  The product detail lists the sizes. Browse cards show the size range and can be filtered by `size=<system>:<code>` or by `my_sizes=true`.
- **Usual sizes (catalogue, per user).** `GET`/`PUT /me/sizes` holds one usual size per system. They are part of the account export and are erased on account deletion.
- **Sized commerce.**
  - **Offers:** per-size stock (`sandbox_offer_size`); for a sized product, `stock` is per size unless `size_stock` is given. The public offer shows which sizes are in stock.
  - **Cart and checkout:** a cart line needs a size for a sized product and none for a one-size product. Checkout locks and decrements the size's stock.
  - **Orders and units:** order lines and the issued units record the size, and cancelling restocks that size.
- **Sized ownership and wardrobe.**
  - The claimed item's presentation carries the unit's size, and staff issuance accepts an optional size.
  - In the wardrobe, every item has a size: the purchased size for verified items, or a user-set size (any item can be corrected).
  - Wardrobe items can be filtered by size and sorted by `sort=size`, which orders by system and rank with unsized items last.
- **Personal recommendations** prefer products available in the user's usual sizes.
- **Pages:**
  - "My sizes" on the account page;
  - a size filter and "In my size" in the store;
  - a size picker on the product page and sizes on the receipt;
  - size sorting and editing in the wardrobe;
  - a sizes panel in the staff product view.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities
- `size-registry`: size systems and values as managed objects, product sizes, assignment rule.
- `sized-commerce`: per-size stock, sized cart lines, checkout, orders, units and cancellation.
- `personal-sizes`: usual sizes per user, store filtering, wardrobe sizes and sorting, size-aware recommendations.

### Modified Capabilities
None. These are additions to the existing behaviour; unsized products keep working unchanged.

## Impact

Migrations `0011_sizes` and `0012_size_browse` (see design D8). New and changed columns:
- new tables: `size_system`, `size_value`, `product_size`, `user_size_preference`, `sandbox_offer_size`;
- `cart_line` gains a size and its primary key changes;
- `sandbox_order_line`, `garment_unit` and `wardrobe_item_state` gain size columns.

Routes are added under `/catalogue`, `/admin/catalogue` and `/me`, and the contracts are regenerated. Existing clients keep working: size fields are optional for one-size products.
