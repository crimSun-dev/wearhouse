# Design

Digital Wardrobe uses the pace of an independent fashion editorial on the landing page and the clarity of a fitting room in the app. Portrait photography, a large serif display voice and an ink-dark rain chapter carry the story. Fine rules, flat controls and warm paper keep the planner readable.

Links and tabs use underlines or rules. Primary actions use solid plum with a modest corner radius. Forms use native controls with persistent labels. Status text includes provenance and never depends on color alone. The landing timeline supports keyboard scrubbing and static scenes with reduced motion. The wardrobe picker stacks below the avatar on mobile, with an explicit open and close control.
