# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Vite and TypeScript, with a Three.js avatar and a same-origin FastAPI backend.

## Users

Indonesian Gen Z students choosing clothes for Jakarta weather and an occasion. Course examiners follow the weather integration, fallback and garment registration during the INFS4305 live demo.

## Product Purpose

Help users dress for the forecast with clothes they own, try an outfit on an avatar, save a plan and register a purchase from its tag.

## Operating Context

Digital Wardrobe is a Sampoerna University capstone and system integration project. The FastAPI server owns ranking, storage and BMKG access. The public editorial story leads into the live planner.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Date and occasion selection, ranked suggestions, outfit composition, week planning, QR lookup and one-time activation. Weather provenance, manual fallback and interface logs remain visible. All people and wardrobe data in the demo are synthetic.
