# Digital Wardrobe landing brief

Confirmed direction: editorial fashion story. Use the supplied frontend brief for the visual and interaction sequence.

- Audience: Jakarta students and Sampoerna University examiners.
- Journey: landing introduction, weather mismatch, forecast timeline, integration and fallback, tag registration, week planning, app entry.
- Pace: quiet open, direct problem statement, afternoon rain as the visual peak, clear operating details and a calm close.
- Peak: an ordinary campus look turns into a rain-ready outfit as Hujan Ringan arrives.
- Signature move: on wide screens scroll advances the pinned weather scene. The range control makes the same scenes available by keyboard and on handheld screens.
- Style: independent fashion editorial with warm paper, plum ink and original Jakarta rooftop imagery.
- Structure: a sequence of distinct scenes in a shared editorial world.
- Assets: the supplied mockups as functional references and two original synthetic rooftop portraits for clear and rainy weather.

Visitors should leave knowing why the forecast changes their outfit and where to open the wardrobe.
